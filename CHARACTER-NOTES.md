# Blanta — Character Notes

## Established character

Blanta, aka Black Santa, stars in a short, funny seasonal picture-book concept. His appearance is based on the creator's likeness: an athletic adult Black man with a short textured haircut, faded sides, and a full dark beard. Keep his eyes naturally aligned and his expressions warm and playful.

The illustration style uses expressive ink linework, warm painted textures, and cozy holiday settings.

## The three-gift tradition

Blanta always gives each child exactly three gifts:

1. A toy.
2. A Black / urban item.
3. Something that will help their future.

These three categories are established by the creator. The particular gifts vary with the child's interests and story. The second category can celebrate Black culture, creativity, style, or community through a meaningful, age-appropriate item. The future gift supports an interest, skill, opportunity, or longer-term goal.

## Current visual example

The v3 concept sheet shows one example trio: a green toy truck, a comic featuring an original Black superhero, and a beginner robotics kit. These examples are illustration choices, not mandatory gifts for every child.

The scene includes a friendly chubby pit bull with a short gray coat, white chest and toes, and natural ears. The dog's name and ownership have not been established.

## Proposed recurring line

“One for fun. One for the culture. One for your future.”

This line is a proposal for review, not finalized manuscript text.
